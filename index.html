About me

More Than Just What I Build

Tech is my craft, and solving real African problems is my greatest obsession.

Founder / Developer

Moyebi Ayodele Oluwasegun

My name is Ayodele, a fullstack developer and product founder based in Abuja with a deep focus on both the frontend and backend. I work with Next.js, React Native, and Supabase, but where I truly come alive is in the details of a product that actually works for real people.

It started with a question: why don't Africans have event tech that works for them, not against them? That question led me to build EventPass, and I haven't stopped building since.

For me it's always been about the craft. The experiences that feel right, the interfaces people use without thinking. That obsession is what shapes every product I touch, from Nera's hyperlocal commerce flows to the client sites we ship at Mouse Technologies.

I'm open to meaningful collaborations. If you're building something that matters for Africa, I'd love to be part of it.
