What should a portfolio website include?

A good portfolio website is not a gallery. It is a clear argument for why someone should trust you with work, money, access, or attention.

Build my portfolio→View case study
Core checklist
  • Positioning statement
  • Selected projects
  • About section
  • Testimonials
  • Services or offer
  • Contact path

The sections that matter most.

Hero

Say your role, who you help, and what kind of work you want in plain language.

Projects

Show context, role, decisions, visuals, and outcomes. Screenshots alone are rarely enough.

Proof

Add testimonials, client logos, live links, press, GitHub, or results that make your claims believable.
