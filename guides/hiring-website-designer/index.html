Client prep

How to prepare before hiring a website designer.

Good preparation makes the project faster, cleaner, and easier to price. You do not need everything perfect, but you should know the basics before design starts.

Fill project intake→View packages
Prepare these
  • Business goals
  • Target audience
  • Core pages
  • Brand assets
  • Examples you like
  • Budget and timeline

What to gather first.

Your offer

Write what you sell, who it is for, what is included, and what makes it different.

Your proof

Collect testimonials, client names, project photos, certificates, results, or live links.

Your content

Prepare logo files, brand colors, photos, service lists, contact details, and preferred examples.
